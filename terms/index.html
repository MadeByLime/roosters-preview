These terms cover what to expect when you book a repair or service visit with Roosters, whether you called us, filled out the callback form, or booked through this website. Roosters is operated by Roosters Service LLC in Pleasanton, California, and serves San Francisco and the Bay Area. By requesting a callback or scheduling a visit, you agree to the terms below.

Agreement to these terms

Using this website, submitting the callback form, or booking a visit by phone means you accept these terms. If you don't agree with something here, please don't submit the form or book a visit, and call us instead so we can talk it through.

Our services

Roosters provides appliance repair across the Bay Area, along with handyman work and cleaning. A licensed, insured technician visits your home or property to diagnose and fix the problem you've described. We work on most major appliance brands; coverage can vary by brand and by the specific appliance, and we'll tell you plainly if something is outside what we can service.

Scheduling and callbacks

When you leave your name and number through the callback form, a real person calls you back within 15 minutes during business hours, Monday through Friday, 8AM to 8PM. We'll offer the soonest visit we have, often the same or next day, though that depends on the day's schedule. A booked visit is a time window, not an exact minute, and we'll call if we're running behind.

Diagnostic visits and pricing

Every repair starts with a flat $140 diagnostic visit. That fee covers the technician's trip and their assessment of what's wrong. If you go ahead with the repair, the $140 is credited toward the labor, so you're not paying for the diagnosis twice.

Estimates and approval

After the diagnostic, your technician quotes the repair on the spot, before any work starts. We won't begin a repair you haven't approved. If the job turns out to need something beyond the original quote once it's opened up, we'll stop and talk to you about the added cost before continuing.

Payment

Payment is due when the work is finished, unless you've set up invoiced billing with us as a property manager. We accept card, cash, check, or Zelle. Whatever's easiest for you works for us.

Parts and warranty

Parts we install carry the manufacturer's warranty, and we source them only from vetted, licensed suppliers. If a part fails under that warranty, tell us and we'll help sort it out. This warranty covers the part itself; it doesn't cover a new, unrelated problem that shows up later.

Cancellations and rescheduling

Plans change. Call or text us as soon as you know you need to move or cancel a visit, and we'll get you rebooked. Giving us notice before the technician is already on the way helps us offer that time slot to another customer waiting on a repair.

Using our website

This website is here to help you learn about our services and get in touch. Please don't use it to submit false information, attempt to disrupt the site, or copy its content for your own commercial use without asking us first.

Limitation of liability

We carry insurance and stand behind our work. That said, Roosters isn't liable for indirect losses, like spoiled food from a fridge that failed before we could get to it, beyond what our insurance and applicable law provide for. If something about a repair goes wrong, tell us directly first so we can make it right.

Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of California. If a disagreement comes up that we can't resolve directly, it will be handled in the courts of California, unless the law requires otherwise.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as our business or the law changes. When we do, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top of the page.

Homeowner questions,
answered.

Is there a service call fee?

Yes. A flat $140 assessment visit covers the technician's on-site diagnosis, a written scope of work, and a repair quote. Approve the repair and that $140 comes off the labor.

Will I know the cost before you start?

Yes. We explain the scope and provide a written estimate before you approve the work. If a job may involve additional costs, we discuss how those will be handled in advance and keep you informed of any changes.

Can I ask you to handle several things around the house?

Absolutely. Send us your list, even if it includes different types of work. We'll confirm what we can help with and how to organize it. Some tasks can be combined in one visit; others may need separate appointments.

Do I need to buy parts or materials myself?

We can help source parts and choose suitable materials for the work. If you already have something purchased or a particular product in mind, let us know beforehand so we can check what's needed and plan accordingly.

How soon can you come out?

Availability depends on the work, your location, and our schedule. Tell us what needs attention and your preferred timing, and we'll confirm the earliest available appointment. If parts or materials need to be ordered, we'll explain how that affects the next steps.

Do I need to be home during the visit?

We arrange access with you before the appointment. If you won't be home, let us know in advance so we can confirm whether the work can go ahead and what arrangements are needed.

How should I prepare for the appointment?

Clear a path to the work area, move fragile or personal items nearby, and keep pets away from the space where we'll be working. Photos and a short description of the issue beforehand also help us prepare for your visit.

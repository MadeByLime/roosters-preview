How appliance repair works with Roosters.

  1. Call and schedule your service

    Our service coordinator answers your call and schedules a same-day or next-day appointment, subject to availability. You receive a two-hour arrival window. If we miss your call, we return it within 15 minutes.

  2. Technician arrival and diagnosis

    Your technician arrives within the scheduled window and inspects the appliance to identify the cause of the problem. The diagnostic visit costs a flat $140.

  3. Repair estimate and approval

    After diagnosing the issue, your technician provides an estimate covering parts and labor. Repairs begin only with your approval. If you proceed, the $140 diagnostic fee is deducted from the labor charge.

  4. On-site repair or parts ordering

    The van carries common parts, so some repairs are finished on the first visit. If the part you need isn't on board, we order it. That usually takes 3 to 5 business days, and your technician comes back to install it.

  5. Parts and labor warranty

    We use genuine manufacturer parts and back our repairs with a 90-day parts and labor warranty. If the same fault returns during that period, we return to resolve it at no additional charge.

Before you book.

Do you charge for the visit?

A flat $140. The tech diagnoses the fault and quotes the repair during that visit, and if you go ahead the $140 comes off the labor. Decide against it and the $140 is all you owe.

How soon can someone be here?

Same day or the next one, depending on how full the schedule already is. You get a two-hour arrival window, not an all-day wait.

Will it be fixed in one visit?

Sometimes. A few faults need no part at all, and the van carries a small stock of the ones that fail most often. More often, the part has to be ordered. That takes three to five business days, then we come back and fit it. You’ll know which it is before the tech leaves.

Which brands do you work on?

Everyday brands, and the built-in ones Bay Area kitchens are full of: Sub-Zero, Wolf, Thermador, Bosch, and Bertazzoni. Miele is the one we turn down. Call with your model number and you’ll have an answer in a minute.

Is the repair covered afterwards?

90 days on both the parts and the labor. Parts are genuine manufacturer parts from licensed suppliers, nothing aftermarket. If the same fault comes back inside those 90 days, so do we.

What if the repair isn’t worth it?

Then we say so. Sometimes the quote lands close to the price of a new machine, and paying to fix an old one makes no sense. You’ll leave the visit knowing exactly what failed and what it would have taken, which is worth having before you go shopping.

I rent. Can you still come?

If you want the building to pay for it, start with your property manager. They may have their own vendor under contract. If you’re covering the repair yourself, call us directly and we’ll book it with you.

My building wants a certificate of insurance.

We’ll send one. Tell the office which building and who it’s addressed to when you book.

Before you book a cleaning.

Straight answers. Anything else, call and ask.

How do you price a cleaning?

By the work in front of us. More rooms cost more, and a light clean costs less than one with built-up grime or set-in stains. You get the price before we start.

Do I need to supply products or equipment?

No. We bring our own quality products and equipment, including organic, safe-to-use options.

How many cleaners will come?

As many as the job needs. A small apartment may take one cleaner, a larger home two or three.

What if something gets missed?

If something we agreed on was missed, we come back and finish it at no extra charge.

Do you offer weekly or biweekly cleaning?

We focus on one-time, deep and move-in or move-out cleaning, plus windows. Call and tell us what you need.

Is there anything you do not clean?

We do not take deep carpet cleaning, biohazard or trauma cleanup, hoarding cleanouts, mold remediation, or heavy post-construction cleanup. Those need specialists.

Are you insured?

Yes. Roosters carries general liability insurance, and every cleaner we send is vetted.

What areas do you serve?

San Francisco and 48 more cities across San Mateo, Alameda, Santa Clara, and Contra Costa counties. Marin is not in our service area. Call with your address and we will confirm coverage.

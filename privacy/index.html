This policy explains what happens to the information you give Roosters when you ask us to fix an appliance. It covers our website, the callback form, and the calls and texts we exchange while we schedule and finish your repair. Roosters is operated by Roosters Service LLC in Pleasanton, California, and serves San Francisco and the Bay Area.

Information we collect

We collect the details you send us and the details we need to reach you back. In most cases that means:

  • Your name and phone number.
  • Your service address, so a technician can find the job.
  • An email address, if you choose to give one.
  • What is wrong with the appliance: the type, the brand, and anything you tell us about the problem through the callback form or on the phone.

Our website may also record basic technical information that most sites log, such as your browser type and the pages you view. We use this to keep the site working and to understand which pages people find useful. We do not ask you for a Social Security number, a bank account number, or any similar sensitive identifier through this website.

How we use it

We use your information to do the work you asked for. That includes:

  • Calling or texting you back to confirm details and set a time.
  • Scheduling a visit and routing a technician to your address.
  • Quoting the repair and letting you know about parts and warranty.
  • Keeping a record of the service in case you need us again.

We may also contact you about a repair we have already done, for example to follow up on a warranty question. We do not use your information to build a profile of you for advertising.

We do not sell your information

Roosters does not sell your personal information, and we do not rent or trade it. We share it only where sharing is part of doing the job, which is described in the next section.

Service providers

Running a repair business takes a few outside tools. We may use trusted companies to host our website, deliver our text messages and calls, and help us schedule jobs and keep records. These providers only handle your information to perform work for us, and they are expected to protect it and to use it for no other purpose.

We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our customers and our team.

Text messages and calls

When you give us your phone number through the callback form or over the phone, you agree that we can call and text you about your repair. These messages are about your job: confirming a time, letting you know a technician is on the way, or answering a question. Message and data rates from your carrier may apply.

You can opt out at any time. Reply STOP to a text to stop texts, or tell us on a call, and we will reach you another way. We do not sell or share your phone number for anyone else's marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to serve you and to run the business responsibly. That includes finishing your repair, honoring a warranty, keeping accurate records, and meeting our tax and legal obligations. When we no longer need it, we take reasonable steps to remove or de-identify it.

Your California privacy rights

California residents have rights over their personal information under state law. You can:

  • Ask what personal information we have collected about you.
  • Ask us to delete personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong.
  • Tell us not to sell or share your information. We already do not sell it, so there is nothing to opt out of, but you are welcome to confirm this with us.

We will not treat you differently for using these rights. To make a request, contact us with the details below. We may need to confirm your identity before we act, so that we release information only to the person it belongs to.

Children's privacy

Our services are meant for adults arranging home repairs. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our business changes or as the law changes. When we do, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top of the page.

Contact us

Questions about your privacy, or want to make a request? Reach us directly.

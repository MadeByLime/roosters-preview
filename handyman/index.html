Questions we get a lot.

Short answers. If yours isn't here, call and ask.

What does the $140 service call cover?

The service call starts at $140. It is first of all a diagnostic visit: the technician looks at the job, works out the full scope, and gives you a firm estimate before anything starts. If a small fix can be finished during that visit, it can be included. If you go ahead with the work, the service call is credited toward it.

How do you price larger handyman jobs?

We review the task, materials, access, and time required, then give you an estimate before work begins. Faucet replacement, larger installations, and multi-step repairs are priced individually.

Can I put several small jobs on one list?

Yes. Send the complete list with enough detail for us to prepare. We will confirm what fits into the appointment and tell you if anything needs a separate estimate or return trip.

Do you bring materials?

We carry common hardware and supplies. If your job needs a specific fixture, finish, or part, we confirm it with you before the appointment or include it in the estimate.

What areas do you serve?

We serve San Francisco and 48 more cities across San Mateo, Alameda, Santa Clara, and Contra Costa counties. Marin is not in our service area. Call with your address and we will confirm coverage.

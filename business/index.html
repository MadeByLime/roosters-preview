Answers for property managers.

How soon can you visit a property?

Same-day or next-day appointments may be available, depending on the work, location, and schedule. Tell us what needs attention and how urgent it is. We'll confirm the earliest available visit and discuss anything that could affect completion, such as parts availability.

What happens if the cost changes after the estimate?

We work to the quoted price. Where needed, we agree on an allowance for unforeseen issues in advance and keep you informed of adjustments. If a change would exceed that allowance, we explain the issue and provide revised pricing for your review.

What updates will I receive about the work?

You receive a detailed service report covering our findings, completed work, and any next steps. We include photos when needed and call when something requires discussion, so you have clear information to share with the property owner.

Do I need to be at the property during the visit?

You don't need to attend if access can be arranged with the resident or your on-site contact. Share the relevant contact details, entry instructions, and any building restrictions beforehand. We'll coordinate the visit and keep you informed.

Can you work around a scheduled move-in?

Share the target move-in date and work list as early as possible. We'll review the scope, coordinate the order of repairs and cleaning, and confirm what can be completed within the available time.

How is work across multiple properties billed?

Work is itemized by property and work order, with a monthly statement to help you track expenses across your portfolio. We confirm billing arrangements when setting up your account and keep your property details and billing preferences on file.

How does invoicing work?

We issue an itemized invoice after each job is completed and approved. Each invoice identifies the property and work performed, making it easy to track expenses across your portfolio.

Is there a service call fee?

Yes. A $140 assessment visit covers the technician's on-site diagnosis and a written scope of work for the property.

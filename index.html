Do you charge for a diagnosis?

Yes, a flat $140 diagnostic visit. If you go ahead with the repair, that $140 is credited toward the labor.

How soon can you come out?

Often the same or next day, sometimes about two days, depending on the day’s schedule. We won’t promise a slot we can’t keep.

Do the repairs have a warranty?

Parts carry the manufacturer’s warranty. We source them only from vetted, licensed suppliers.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes to both. Our license number is 50567.

Which brands do you service?

Most major brands, though coverage varies by brand and appliance. We’re an authorized Bertazzoni service provider. Call and we’ll confirm yours in a minute.

Where do you work?

San Francisco and the greater Bay Area, including San Mateo, Alameda, Santa Clara, and Contra Costa counties.

How do I pay?

Whatever’s easiest for you: card, cash, check, or Zelle.
